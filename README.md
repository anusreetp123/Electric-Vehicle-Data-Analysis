## Key Insights
### BEVs Dominate the Market:-

Battery Electric Vehicles represent 77.6% of all EVs, signaling a decisive 
consumer and policy shift away from hybrids toward full electrification.

### Tesla Is the Undisputed Leader:-

With 52.7% market share and the top two models, Tesla's ecosystem 4 from 
charging to software 4 sets the competitive benchmark.

### Adoption Is Accelerating:-

Registrations peaked at ~37K in 2023, with strong year-over-year growth since 
2017 driven by expanding model availability and federal incentives.

### Policy & Incentives Drive Demand:-

State mandates, federal tax credits, and infrastructure investment are directly 
correlated with regional adoption rates 4 particularly in washington and the West.
